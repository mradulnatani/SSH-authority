To use your certificate with SSH:
1. Submit your SSH public key (e.g. ~/.ssh/id_ed25519.pub)
2. Download the signed certificate as <key>-cert.pub
3. Restrict permissions: chmod 600 ~/.ssh/id_ed25519-cert.pub
4. Connect: ssh -i ~/.ssh/id_ed25519 <role>@<server-ip>
